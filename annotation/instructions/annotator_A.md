# Instructions for Annotator A (500 items, verify)

Thank you for helping. You will check romanized Kannada written by an AI model (Gemini): is each version fine, or
wrong? If it is wrong, you type a fix. This file replaces the one sent earlier; please use only this one.

**What you need:** `annotator_A.xlsx` and Excel (laptop or desktop if you can; the Excel phone app also works).

## First: the 3 examples (reply within a day)
At the top of the sheet **items** there are 3 shaded rows marked EXAMPLE (not among your items): one "fine", one
"wrong" with a fix, and one "wrong" because the meaning changed. Look at them and reply to Aarnav "examples ok", or
send your corrections, within a day. Then start the items.

## Before you start
- Turn off AutoCorrect in Excel: File > Options > Proofing > AutoCorrect Options, and untick "Correct TWo INitial
  CApitals", "Capitalize first letter of sentences" and "Replace text as you type".
- On a phone or tablet, also turn off autocorrect and automatic capitals in your keyboard settings.

## For each item (one row per item)
You see the Kannada sentence, its English translation and two Gemini versions:
- **Gemini natural:** the Kannada typed in English letters, word for word, the way people type on a phone.
- **Gemini Kanglish:** how a young person in Bengaluru would write the same message on WhatsApp, with English words
  mixed in where that is natural.

For each of the two versions:
1. In **OK?** choose **fine** if it is how people would type it on WhatsApp and it keeps the meaning.
2. Otherwise choose **wrong** and type your fix in **fix**: the whole sentence the way it should be.
3. Leave **fix** empty when the version is fine. Do not change a version just to match your own spelling: there is no
   single correct spelling.

Only the yellow cells can be changed. Use **Note** for anything odd (for example, a Kannada sentence that is itself
wrong).

## Rules
- No AI or translation tools, and no dictionaries.
- Do not discuss the items with anyone else working on this project.

## Pace and sending your work
- About 20 seconds per item, about 3 hours in total. Short sessions are fine: save (Ctrl+S) and close.
- Always save as an Excel Workbook (.xlsx), never as CSV.
- Send the .xlsx file to Aarnav on WhatsApp (as a Document) every week.
