# Instructions for Annotator B (100 items, in two parts)

Thank you for helping. You will write romanized Kannada yourself, and later check versions written by an AI model.

**What you need:** Excel (on a laptop or desktop if you can; the Excel phone app also works). Part 1 is the file
`annotator_B_part1.xlsx`. Part 2 comes later, as a second file from Aarnav.

## Before you type anything
- Turn off AutoCorrect in Excel: File > Options > Proofing > AutoCorrect Options, and untick "Correct TWo INitial
  CApitals", "Capitalize first letter of sentences" and "Replace text as you type".
- On a phone or tablet, also turn off autocorrect and automatic capitals in your keyboard settings.
- Type your name in the yellow cell on the sheet **READ_ME**. Use exactly the same name in both parts.

## Part 1: 40 items (file annotator_B_part1.xlsx)
In the sheet "items" you see only a Kannada sentence in each row. Write two versions of it yourself, from scratch, in
the yellow cells:
- **Natural romanization:** the Kannada typed in Latin letters, the way you would type it on a phone, word for word.
  Keep English words that are already in the Kannada as they are.
- **Kanglish:** how a young person in Bengaluru would write the same message on WhatsApp, with English words mixed in
  where that is natural. Keep the full meaning.

There is nothing to rate in Part 1. Do not look anything up.

**When all 40 are done, save the file and send it to Aarnav straight away.** Aarnav then sends Part 2.

## Part 2: 60 items (file annotator_B_part2.xlsx, sent later)
First read `worked_examples.xlsx` (3 examples, not among your items). Then, in the sheet "items", you see the Kannada
sentence, its English translation and two AI drafts. For each draft:
1. **Rate it first, before changing anything**, with the dropdowns: naturalness from 1 (nobody would type this) to 5
   (exactly how people type), and whether it keeps the meaning (yes or no).
2. **Then correct it** in the column "your corrected version", which starts as a copy of the draft. If it is already
   fine, leave it unchanged.

Only the yellow cells can be changed; the rest is locked so nothing is deleted by accident.

## Rules
- Write the way you would really type. There is no single correct spelling.
- Do not use translation tools, AI tools or dictionaries.
- Do not discuss items with anyone else working on this project, and do not look at anyone else's work.
- Use the Note column for anything odd.

## Sending your work
- Always save as an Excel Workbook (.xlsx), never as CSV, and send the .xlsx on WhatsApp (attach it as a Document).
- About 2 minutes per item. If something does not work, ask Aarnav.
