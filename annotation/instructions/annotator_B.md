# Instructions for Annotator B (100 items, in two parts)

Thank you for helping. Part 1 replaces the file sent earlier; please use only the new one.

**What you need:** Excel (laptop or desktop if you can; the Excel phone app also works).

## Before you start
- Turn off AutoCorrect in Excel: File > Options > Proofing > AutoCorrect Options, and untick "Correct TWo INitial
  CApitals", "Capitalize first letter of sentences" and "Replace text as you type".
- On a phone or tablet, also turn off autocorrect and automatic capitals in your keyboard settings.

## Part 1: 40 sentences, your own way (file annotator_B_part1.xlsx)
Each row has a Kannada sentence and its English translation. Write it yourself, from scratch:
- **Your natural:** the Kannada typed in English letters, the way you would type it on WhatsApp, word for word.
- **Your Kanglish:** how a young person in Bengaluru would write the same message on WhatsApp, with English words
  mixed in where that is natural. Keep the full meaning.

There is no single correct spelling: type it the way you really would. Do not look anything up. About 1.5 hours.
**When all 40 are done, save and send the file to Aarnav straight away.** Part 2 comes after that.

## Part 2: 60 checks (file annotator_B_part2.xlsx, sent later)
The same task as Annotator A. Each row has the Kannada sentence, the English, and two versions written by an AI model
(Gemini). The 3 shaded EXAMPLE rows at the top show how. For each version:
1. In **OK?** choose **fine** if it is how people would type it on WhatsApp and it keeps the meaning.
2. Otherwise choose **wrong** and type your fix in **fix** (the whole sentence the way it should be). Leave **fix**
   empty when it is fine.

## Rules
- No AI or translation tools, and no dictionaries.
- Do not discuss the items with anyone else working on this project, and do not look at anyone else's work.
- Use the Note column for anything odd.

## Sending your work
- Always save as an Excel Workbook (.xlsx), never as CSV, and send the .xlsx on WhatsApp (as a Document).
